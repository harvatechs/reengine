## Parallel Execution Map (For Multi-Agent Teams)

* After **Phase 1** exits, **Phase 2 (`@reengine/providers`)** and **Phase 3 (`@reengine/mcp-rea`)** have zero mutual dependencies and can be built concurrently by two specialized agents.
* **Phase 5 (`@reengine/ui`)** can also begin immediately after **Phase 1** using a deterministic `MockAgentEventBus` emitting synthetic streaming/tool/evidence events, then wire into the real Phase 4 orchestrator cleanly at integration.
